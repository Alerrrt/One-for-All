# Product Guidelines: File Conversion Engine

## 1. Visual Identity and Branding

The File Conversion Engine should project a **Modern & Minimalist** visual identity. This will be achieved through:

*   **Clean Design:** A clutter-free interface with a focus on the core functionality of file conversion.
*   **Simple Color Palette:** A limited and cohesive color scheme that is easy on the eyes and reinforces a modern aesthetic.
*   **Functional Focus:** The design should prioritize usability and make the conversion process as intuitive as possible.

## 2. Tone of Voice

The application's tone of voice should be **Friendly & Supportive**. This means:

*   **Empathetic Language:** Communications should be understanding and encouraging, especially when guiding users through the conversion process or handling errors.
*   **Helpful Guidance:** The application should provide clear and helpful instructions and feedback to the user at every step.
*   **Approachable and Positive:** The overall tone should be positive and approachable, making the user feel comfortable and confident in using the tool.
